Delete Your Account — Retro Arcade

Retro Arcade is made by Orbit Oryx (Orbit Oryx for Software). Deleting your account removes everything we store about you. Here is exactly how, and exactly what happens.

Option 1 — inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Retro Arcade
  2. Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon on the home screen
  3. Scroll to the Account section
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm

Deletion is immediate.

Option 2 — by email

Email support@orbitoryx.com with the subject "Delete my Retro Arcade account" and include your player name. We complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your player name (nickname)
  • Your anonymous account identifier
  • All of your game scores and leaderboard entries

That is everything — we never collect email addresses, real names, locations, or contacts, so there is nothing else to delete.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Your purchase record ("Unlock all games") remains with Google Play / the App Store under their own policies — it belongs to your store account, not to us — so you can restore your purchase if you return. Settings stored only on your device (sound, language) are removed when you uninstall the app.
